Taption Encoder test video samples

Most samples in this folder were generated as short codec-specific clips from:
Big Buck Bunny, Blender Foundation, https://download.blender.org/peach/bigbuckbunny_movies/BigBuckBunny_320x180.mp4

Big Buck Bunny project license reference:
https://peach.blender.org/about/

The Theora sample was copied from:
https://download.blender.org/peach/trailer/trailer_400p.ogg

The Sorenson Video 3 sample was trimmed from the FFmpeg samples archive:
https://samples.ffmpeg.org/V-codecs/SVQ3/Vertical400kbit.sorenson3.mov

The Microsoft Video 1, ASUS V1, and ASUS V2 samples were generated locally from
the Taption Encoder fixture script:
Scripts/generate_video_codec_fixtures.sh

Generated and trimmed files are renamed with the codec name only.
