Taption Encoder bundled test video samples

Policy for this folder:
- Each bundled video sample must be 1 MB or smaller.
- Samples should use official/public test-media sources. When the official
  source file is larger than 1 MB, the bundled file may be a trimmed or
  same-codec transcode derived from that official source.
- No purely local/generated-only sample remains after the 2026-06-28 source
  audit.
