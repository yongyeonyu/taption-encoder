Taption Encoder bundled test video samples

Policy for this folder:
- Each bundled video sample is normalized to approximately 5 seconds for
  queue thumbnail and preview verification.
- Samples should stay 1 MB or smaller when the codec/container can reasonably
  support that at 5 seconds. Fixed-bitrate DV-family samples are the current
  documented exception: `DV.dv` and `DVCPro HD.mov` preserve the expected codec
  identity and 5-second duration, so they exceed 1 MB.
- Samples should use official/public test-media sources when available. When
  the official source file is larger than 1 MB, the bundled file may be a
  trimmed, looped, or same-codec transcode derived from that official source.
- Generated-only test-pattern fixtures are not bundled. If no official/public
  source file is found for an executable codec, the codec remains unsupported
  by the default sample bundle until a real source is available.
- When source media includes readable audio, bundled derivatives preserve that
  source audio content through codec/container-appropriate re-encoding. Samples
  without readable source audio may carry generated preview audio; current
  no-audio exceptions are raw `H.261.h261` and `RoQ Video.roq`.
