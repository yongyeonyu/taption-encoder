PUBLIC PRODUCT PAGE COPY

Product name
Bokki Encoder

Headline
Convert videos on your iPhone or iPad.

Short introduction
Bring videos in from Photos, Files, or a folder. Convert supported videos to H.265 (HEVC), choose a resolution and target file size, and save the result as MP4 or MOV. Bokki Encoder runs video conversion on your device and gives you a practical workflow for storage and sharing.

For whom
For people who want to prepare, resize, or convert videos on their iPhone or iPad.

Feature 1 — Start with your own files
Choose videos from Photos or Files, or open a folder to work through multiple supported videos in sequence.

Feature 2 — Export H.265 in MP4 or MOV
Convert supported source videos to H.265 (HEVC) and choose MP4 or MOV as the output container. Available input formats and settings depend on the source file and device.

Feature 3 — Set resolution and target size
Keep the original resolution or choose an available output resolution. Set a target file size to suit your storage and sharing needs. Target size is an estimate; the final size depends on the video and conversion settings.

Feature 4 — Preview before converting
Play and inspect a video before starting conversion, so you can check the source you are working with.

Feature 5 — Convert locally, then save where you choose
Video processing runs on your device. Save the converted result to Photos or a selected folder. Bokkiyaw does not operate a server that receives your source or converted video files through the app.

How it works
1. Choose videos from Photos, Files, or a folder.
2. Select MP4 or MOV, then set the output resolution and target size.
3. Check the required Stars and start conversion.
4. Save the result to Photos or your chosen folder.

Release status
Available now on the App Store. Free to download for iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.

Stars and ads note
The app is ad-supported. Conversion uses Stars, and rewarded ads let you earn Stars when ads are available. The current release does not offer a paid Stars purchase flow.
