What this screen does

The four principles

1

One decision per state. Add, choose, run, done. The screen never asks two questions at once.

2

Show the outcome, not the parameter. People don't want 1920×1080; they want 6.1 MB. Every control carries its result.

3

Thumb zone. The one primary action is a 56pt button docked at the bottom, above the ad — never below it.

4

Standard iOS only. Sheets for options, swipe to delete, long-press for details, share sheet for output. No invented gestures.

What was removed

  • Path HW / SW, Readiness, Audio Copy, Probe — engine vocabulary, moved to Diagnostics.
  • Second banner ad — one banner, below the primary action, never above it.
  • Full-width coral Exit — a destructive-looking button for a harmless action. Now under ⋯.
  • Gear icon — settings are a value you tap, not a mechanism you operate.
  • Nine stacked layers — now a title, a list, one card, one button.

Design tokens

Extends the existing TaptionTheme rather than replacing it, so the change ships incrementally.
